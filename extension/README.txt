Tally for Canvas
================
Reads your own Canvas (while you are signed in) and brings your classes, assignment
sections, weights and grades into Tally. Nothing is sent to any server.

Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc):
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it.
2. Open chrome://extensions (edge://extensions in Edge) and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.
4. Open your Canvas, click the Tally icon in the toolbar, and press "Sync with Tally".

The first time you sync a Canvas site the browser asks permission to read that one site.
After that, visiting Canvas refreshes your grades in the background (at most every 20 minutes).
